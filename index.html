Tooters Privacy Policy

Effective / last updated: November 20, 2026

Tooters is a small, friends-only app for logging and sharing “toots” with people you choose to add. It is operated by Blink Studio. This page explains what the app collects, why, who it's shared with, and how you can get it deleted. There’s no sign-up with your real name, email, or phone number, and your content is never sold or shared with advertisers.

Information we collect

  • An anonymous account ID. Signing up doesn’t ask for an email, password, or phone number — the app creates a random, anonymous account ID the first time you open it, and that’s what signs you back in on later launches. The session token is stored only on your device.
  • Your profile. The username and avatar color you choose, plus a shareable friend code derived from your account ID.
  • Friend connections. Who you’ve sent or accepted friend requests with.
  • Activity you log. A severity rating, an optional note, the time you logged it, and any emoji reactions on it.
  • On-device preferences. Things like your chosen notification sound and whether notifications are on. These stay in iOS's local storage on your device and are never sent to our servers.

What we don't collect

  • No analytics or advertising SDKs are built into the app, so we don't receive device identifiers (like an IDFA) or usage analytics.
  • No sign-in through Apple, Google, Facebook, or any other third-party identity provider. Authentication is handled entirely by our database.
  • No payment information, since the app has no purchases.

How we use this information

  • To run the app’s core features: your feed, friend requests, and reactions.
  • Notifications. If you turn on “Notify me when friends post,” you'll get a push notification.
  • To apply basic abuse prevention, like blocking a short list of offensive usernames and limiting how many times a friend request can be resent.

Third parties involved

Your account and toot data is stored and processed by Supabase, a database hosting provider that acts on our behalf (a processor, not an independent user of your data). Supabase infrastructure is hosted in data centers that may be located outside your own country — currently the United States. By using the app, you consent to your information being processed there.

Who can see your information

Your activity and profile are visible only to people you’ve added as friends (and to you). We don’t sell your information or share it with advertisers, and there's no public profile or discovery feature.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit between the app and Supabase (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your data is restricted at the database level. There is also no reason for this app to be a meaningful target, since it stores no financial, government ID, or health information. If we ever have a breach, we'll notify affected users and the appropriate authority where required.

Your choices and rights

  • Mute or remove a friend at any time from Settings.
  • Turn off notifications at any time from Settings.
  • Request a copy of your data. There's no in-app export yet — email us (below) and we'll send you what we have tied to your account.
  • Delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete my account. This permanently removes your profile, friend connections, and every toot you’ve logged.

If you're in the EU/UK or California, these map to rights you already have under GDPR or the CCPA — access, correction, deletion, and portability of your personal information. Contact us (below) to exercise any of them; as a small independent developer we handle these manually rather than through an automated portal.

Account recovery

There's no password and no linked email, so there's no "forgot password" flow. Your account lives in your device's local session storage. If you uninstall the app or erase your device without restoring a backup, that session can't be recovered, and a fresh install creates a brand-new, unconnected account. Moving to a new device via an iCloud or computer backup of your old one may carry the session over automatically, though that isn't guaranteed, and we haven't tested it ourselves.

Data retention and deletion

Your profile, friend code, username, and activity are kept for as long as your account exists. There's no automatic expiry. Deleting your account (Settings → Delete my account) removes this data from our live database immediately. Our database may retain the deleted data in routine infrastructure backups for a limited period before those backups are automatically cycled out; we don't separately extract or restore your data from those backups once you've deleted your account.

Children’s privacy

Tooters isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request to access/delete your data, can be sent to support.blinkstudio@gmail.com.
