Frequently asked questions

I'm not getting notifications when friends post.
Check Settings → make sure “Notify me when friends post” is on, and that Tooters has notification permission in iOS Settings → Tooters → Notifications.

How do I add a friend?
From Settings, scan their QR code or share your own Toot Code for them to enter.

Do I lose my account if I delete the app?
Yes. Deleting Tooters, erasing your device, or restoring an old backup ends your account for good, since there's no email or password to recover it with. A regular App Store update is different and never touches your account.

How do I delete my account and data?
Settings → Delete my account. This is permanent and removes your profile, friends, and everything you've logged. See our Privacy Policy for details.
